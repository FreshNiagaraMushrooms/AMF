What We Are

The Mushroom Army is a grassroots network of farmers, chefs, volunteers, and everyday people in Niagara working together to reduce food waste, feed hungry neighbours, and restore balance to our local ecosystems.

We grow mushrooms, recover surplus food from restaurants, and share tens of thousands of free meals with the community every year. Our work blends regenerative agriculture, bioacoustics, and community care into one living system that nourishes both people and planet.

🍲

What We Do

Food Recovery

Partnering with chefs and restaurants to rescue perfectly good food that would otherwise be wasted.

Community Feeding

Turning recovered ingredients into healthy meals distributed to shelters, encampments, and anyone who needs them.

Soil Regeneration

Using spent mushroom substrate and composting systems to improve soil health, grow more food, and close the nutrient loop.

Education & Awareness

Hosting workshops, talks, and events to inspire civic engagement, ecological literacy, and mutual aid.

🌍

Why It Matters

Niagara holds some of the best farmland on Earth — over 60% of Canada's Class 1 & 2 soil. But industrial food systems create waste while people still go hungry. The Mushroom Army believes we can fix both problems at once by connecting surplus to need and waste to regeneration.

Every meal we share keeps good food out of landfills, reduces greenhouse gases, and builds community resilience.
